## Warnings (should fix)

### Structured data
- [x] ~~**`AutomotiveBusiness` has no `address` / `priceRange`.**~~ **Withdrawn.** Both are deliberately banned by `tests/rendered-html.test.mjs` (the structured-data test). The operator's real base is Runcorn (Brisbane), so a "Gold Coast" `PostalAddress` would be fabricated, and the business pays sellers, so a `priceRange` would imply the opposite. ### Social / meta
- [ ] **One identical social card for all 22 pages, and it is 966 KB.** `/img/Best-Cash-for-Cars-Gold-Coast.jpg` is 3840×1600 JPEG. WhatsApp and some Messenger clients commonly skip previews above roughly 600 KB, and WhatsApp is how most Australians share a "who'd you use?" link. Export a 1200×630 JPEG (≤150 KB) for `og:image` in `src/lib/seo.ts:28`, and give each guide its own `image` via `pageMeta` using `post.image`.
- [x] **Blog hub title ended with a truncated brand.** *Fixed 17 Oct:* now "Gold Coast Car Selling Guides | Unique Cash For Cars" (52 chars, `src/app/blog/page.tsx:13`).
- [ ] **Short description** on `/where-do-old-junk-cars-go-…` (107 chars) — expand to ~150 with the outcome the reader gets. That guide's last update is March 2024, the oldest indexable page; give it the same refresh the August guides got.
